13. Community-Managed-Fisheries-and-Catch-Quota-Tracking-System
Sector: Governance/Supply Chain

Concept Overview: Sustainable fishing often relies on catch share or quota systems, where a total allowable catch is allocated among a community of fishermen. This project digitizes that system, allowing fishing cooperatives to manage, track, and trade their catch quotas in a transparent and efficient manner. This helps prevent overfishing and provides a clear, auditable record for regulatory bodies.

Stacks-Specific Implementation: The total allowable catch for a season is set within a Clarity smart contract, which then mints a corresponding number of fungible "Quota Tokens." These tokens are distributed to the fishermen. As fish are caught, tokens are "spent" or burned. Fishermen can also trade their unused quota tokens with others in a peer-to-peer marketplace. The entire system is settled on Bitcoin, providing a secure and auditable record that regulators can use to verify compliance with environmental policies.
